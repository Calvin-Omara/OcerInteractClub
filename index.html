About Our Club

Empowering Youth to Lead & Serve

The Interact Club of Ocer Campion Jesuit College brings together student leaders ages 12–18 to develop leadership skills while discovering the power of Rotary's motto, "Service Above Self".

Our Mission

To instill integrity, empathy, and active civic responsibility in young students of Ocer Campion Jesuit College by executing impactful community service projects, supporting educational excellence, and strengthening local partnerships with the Rotary Club of Gulu.

Our Vision

A thriving generation of compassionate, action-oriented young leaders who embody Jesuit values and Rotary principles, creating sustainable solutions to local challenges and inspiring positive social transformation across Northern Uganda.

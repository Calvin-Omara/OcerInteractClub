Who We Are

Fostering Service, Integrity & Fellowship

Our Mission

To bring together young people at Ocer Campion Jesuit College to develop leadership skills through local and international service activities, while understanding the power of Rotary's motto: "Service Above Self".

Our Vision

To nurture compassionate, ethical, and proactive student leaders who serve as catalysts for positive social transformation within Northern Uganda and globally.

Rotary Affiliation

Rotary International Partnership

Interact is Rotary International's service club for young people aged 12 to 18. Sponsored by the Rotary Club of Gulu, our club at Ocer Campion Jesuit College combines Ignatian values of being "Men and Women for Others" with Rotary's global commitment to humanitarian service.
